04

From fabric
to carton.

What an order passes through before it reaches your warehouse.

  1. 01

    Fabric sourcing

    Premium and imported fabric selected for the weight and hand-feel the style calls for.

  2. 02

    Manufacturing

    Produced in our own facility, equipped with current machinery for knitwear at volume.

  3. 03

    Branding & print

    Applied logos, chest and sleeve prints, and digital print — as seen across the catalogue.

  4. 04

    Quality checks

    Every product goes through quality checks before it is cleared for packing.

  5. 05

    Packing

    Poly-bag and box packing, presented for retail shelves or for gifting.

  6. 06

    Bulk despatch

    Set up for wholesalers and bulk buyers, with rates quoted against the order.
